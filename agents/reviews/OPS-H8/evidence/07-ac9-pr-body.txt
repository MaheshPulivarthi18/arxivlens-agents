### Behaviour changes (decided by pm-lead in design review)
1. `ServiceKafkaProducer.publish` and `publish_dlq` now raise `DeliveryError` when the broker hasn't confirmed delivery within 
`DELIVERY_TIMEOUT_S` (12 s; `message.timeout.ms = 10000`), or when `produce` itself fails. Before, they returned silently even if the 
message was never delivered. The processor's handler therefore fails and is retried (then DLQ'd) when `papers.chunked` isn't delivered, 
instead of committing a paper that's never embedded.
2. The DLQ `stack_trace` is now the handler's real traceback. On the consumer path it used to be `NoneType: None`.
3. **Interim, until OPS-H8b:** while the DLQ is unreachable, the worker exits without committing the failed record (`DeliveryError` 
leaves `start` before its commit, and the consumer closes). Compose (`restart: unless-stopped`) restarts it, the record is redelivered, 
and the handler runs 3× again on each restart until the DLQ write is confirmed. That costs time, never a record. OPS-H8b replaces this 
with an in-place DLQ retry.
