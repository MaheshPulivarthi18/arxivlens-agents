## Summary
The code does what the design says, and it fails closed:
- `Supported` is derived from `status` in the only constructor.
- Unverified is never supported.
- Refusal evidence is `unsupported` only when a check ran.
- A fabricated model quote downgrades its citation before `keep`.
- `content` is the raw chunk on the LLM path.
- Provenance is copied without aliasing.

CI is green. In a scratch module the targeted suite passes and `rag.go` coverage is 99.0%. 32 of QA's 35 single-edit mutants are killed.

It FAILs for three reasons:
- Two AC items the ticket requires in the PR body are missing: the AC5 `git grep` and the AC2 per-mutation list.
- ADR-0014's case-sensitive quote match has no test guarding it. A case-insensitive mutant survives, and with that mutant a case-changed model quote is shown as a supported quote.
- AC1 can't be met as written: it needs edits to `synthesis_test.go`, which the ai role isn't allowed to change. This is a `[spec]` finding for pm-lead, not held against the developer.
