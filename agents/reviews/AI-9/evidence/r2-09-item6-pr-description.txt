### What changed
- `services/embedder/api.py`: new `score_pairs()` — same tokenizer call (truncation, `max_length=512`) and same model; pairs sorted by token length (stable), scored in batches of 8 under `torch.inference_mode()`, each score written back to its input position.
- `/internal/rerank` logs one numbers-only line per request: `rerank passages= batches= max_len= tokens= padded_tokens= single_batch_padded_tokens= threads= tokenize_ms= forward_ms= total_ms=`.

- `tests/test_m0.py`: 4 tests (`-k rerank`): mocked order/batching test, endpoint order test, thread-env parsing, and a real-`bge-reranker-base` parity test (3 queries × 20 passages, old single-batch vs new within 1e-4, same ranking; skips if the model can't load).
